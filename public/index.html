Statutory Reference & Site Boundaries

EU261 & Swiss Agreement

Covers all departures from EU/EEA/CH airports plus arrivals into EU/EEA/CH on EU/EEA/CH carriers. Fixed bands: EUR 250 (<=1,500 km), EUR 400 (1,500-3,500 km or intra-EU >1,500 km), EUR 600 (>3,500 km). Arrival delay >=3h triggers cash payout (Sturgeon C-402/07).

UK261 & Turkish SHY-PASSENGER

UK261 pays GBP 220 / GBP 350 / GBP 520 on UK departures or UK/EU carriers into the UK. Turkish SHY-PASSENGER pays EUR 100 domestic and EUR 250 / 400 / 600 international (in TRY at CBRT selling rate) on Turkish departures or Turkish carriers into Turkey.

US DOT & Montreal Convention

US DOT 14 CFR Part 259 mandates automatic prompt cash refunds for cancellations or significant delays (>=3h domestic, >=6h international) when rebooking is declined. Part 250 pays 200%/400% up to USD 775 / USD 1,550 for involuntary bumping. Montreal Convention Art. 22 caps checked baggage liability at 1,519 SDR.

What This Site Does Not Do

FlightRights Desk does not act as a law firm or file court lawsuits, does not rebook or sell flights, does not process airline credit-card refunds, and does not access live private airline PNR databases. For independent airport ATC/weather verification, reroute deltas, NEB portals, and SHA-256 evidence seals, consult our linked cross-origin peer Disruption Ledger.
